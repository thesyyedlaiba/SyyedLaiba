05 — ABOUT

Hi,
I'm Laiba.

still figuring it out ♡

I think I was always going to end up writing.

I spend far too much time noticing little things, wondering why people feel the way they do, and giving entirely ordinary moments far more thought than they probably deserve.

It makes for a rather busy mind.

I'm a writer, a reader, a psychology student, and an eldest daughter. That last part probably tells you enough about how my brain works.

I'm always making something. A poem, a painting, a page of a book, or an idea I absolutely do not have time for but will probably pursue anyway.

This little corner of the internet is where I keep some of those things.

I'm glad you found your way here.
