I wrote a book
and then hid it.

A little story about avoiding the book that knew too much about me.

I wrote a poetry book last year.

I finished the book. I had the cover. I had everything.

And then... I just didn't publish it.

For an entire year.

I kept avoiding it, even though I'd already done the hardest part. I had written the thing. I had formatted the entire book. I even added the page numbers. So, It was all finished. It was all done. but now that I've published it I'm realzing that maybe I was just scared of actually putting it out there.

Because I knew once it's published, it's not just something sitting on my laptop anymore. Other people can read it. Other people can have opinions about it and suddenly something that felt so personal becomes something I've shared with the world.

So hiding it made sense to me.

Until apparently, August 2026 was the year I finally decided to stop being scared and actually do it.
